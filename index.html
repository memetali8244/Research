Research Themes

Short overview — edit freely
🔷

Semiconductor materials

Crystal growth, thin films, and composition–structure relations for functional semiconductors.

🔬

Optical & dielectric characterization

Ellipsometry, transmission/reflectance, Raman/PL, dielectric function, and derived optical constants.

🧱

Nano-/micro-mechanics

Nanoindentation mapping, load dependence, hardness/modulus, and structure–mechanics correlations.

🧩

Functional composites

Hybrid structures including bio-based cellulose matrices with inorganic functional phases.

🧪

Interfaces & coatings

Adhesion, surface modification, and interface engineering for stable, application-ready materials.

⚙️

Alloys & advanced engineering materials

Collaborative work on metallic systems (e.g., AlSi10Mg) and their mechanical/structural performance.
